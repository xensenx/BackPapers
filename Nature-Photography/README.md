#### Nature Photography
This directory is distinct from the rest of the repository, as it contains photographs taken by a nature enthusiast and my personal friend. With their permission, these photographs are shared here for personal use only.
For permissions beyond personal use, please contact the photographer.

#### A Little More About the Photographer

The person behind these photographs is a self-taught photographer with a genuine interest in nature, particularly insects and plant life.
They have a keen eye for spotting subtle movements and capturing shots that look naturally composed. They are not professionally trained, nor do they make a living from photography. 
For now, it's simply something they enjoy doing in their free time.
